Here is a mathematics write-up:

<document>
{{document}}
</document>

A referee reviewed it and reported:

<referee_report>
{{referee findings}}
</referee_report>

Fix the errors the referee identified, making minimal targeted edits; do not otherwise
rewrite the document. Produce the complete corrected document as a single LaTeX file.
Finally, list the errors the referee marked critical, one per line, each line beginning
with FIXED: or UNABLE: according to whether your edits fix it.

There is no human editor in this workflow. Where the guidance says to flag something,
ask, or mark it, record it as a LaTeX comment beginning %% FLAG: at the relevant point
in the document; do not omit the flag and do not silently resolve the issue yourself.
